Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals use fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into giving away sensitive information.

Fake Login Page

Attackers create websites that look like real login pages to steal usernames and passwords.

Urgent Email

Phishing emails often use fear or urgency, such as “Your account will be closed today.”

Suspicious Links

Attackers may hide dangerous links behind buttons or shortened URLs.
